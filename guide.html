Simple Travel Guide

Travel tip: Plan your budget and pack your essentials before leaving!

Things to Pack (Unordered List)

  • Clothes
  • Phone charger
  • Toiletries
  • Medicines

Steps to Plan a Trip (Ordered List)

  1. Choose your destination.
  2. Decide your budget.
  3. Check travel requirements.
  4. Book your tickets and stay.
  5. Pack your bags.
